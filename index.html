Five ways into the same door

Every lock we fit takes all five, and you decide who gets which. A code for the house help. A fingerprint for your tenant. The app for yourself. The key stays in a drawer for the day the power and the Wi-Fi both go.

Fingerprint

Up to a hundred fingers per lock. Children who keep losing keys, house help, your mother when she visits.

Passcode

Give out a code instead of a key. You can set one that only works this weekend, or one that works once and then dies.

IC card

A small tap card. Handy where you'd rather not enrol fingerprints, like staff who change often.

Mechanical key

Every lock still comes with real keys and a real cylinder. Flat battery, dead power, or you simply prefer a key.

Tuya app

Open it from your phone. From the shop, from work, from Mombasa if that's where you happen to be.

One app for every door you own

Our locks run on Tuya Smart. A landlord with six units manages all of them from one screen instead of six sets of keys. We install the app on your phone before we leave and add the first codes with you watching.

  • Open the door from anywhere

    A delivery arrives while you're at work. You unlock from your phone, they leave the parcel inside, you lock it again.

  • Codes that expire on their own

    Set a code that works Friday to Sunday. By Monday morning it's dead, and you never had to remember to change it.

  • A record of every entry

    The app keeps a list of every time the door opened and which fingerprint or code did it. Landlords tend to find this the most useful part.

  • An alarm if someone forces it

    Try to pry the lock and it screams at the door while sending you a notification at the same time.

  • Warning before the battery dies

    You get warnings for weeks before the batteries go flat. Nobody gets locked out of their own house.

How a fitting goes

Under two hours on most doors, from arrival to your first fingerprint. On several of our locks the fitting is already in the price.

We look at the door

Send photos of the door and tell us how thick it is, or we come and look ourselves. Either way you have the price in writing before anything is ordered.

We fit and test it

Old lock out, new one in, alignment checked. Before we pack up we test every way of opening it, the mechanical key included.

We hand it over properly

We set up the app on your phone, enrol your fingerprints and codes with you watching, and show you how to add more later. After that you have a warranty and a number that answers.
